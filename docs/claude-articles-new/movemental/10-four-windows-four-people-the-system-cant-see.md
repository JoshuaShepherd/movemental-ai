# Forming Pastors Who Cannot Leave

A Competency-Based Model for Bivocational Formation in Urban Kenya

By **Esther Wanjiru** · Kairos University

Published the day it was defended · owned by the author · CC BY 4.0

Open access · free to read

Written by a person.

How we know ↓

Most pastoral training assumes a person can leave -- leave the job, the family, the neighborhood -- to be formed somewhere else. The leaders this study follows cannot. They drive minibuses and sell vegetables in Kawangware, and they are pastoring anyway. This project asks how a person is formed faithfully _inside_ the work they cannot leave.

### What the model holds

In a competency-based account, formation is assessed across content, character, and craftDefined once · across the networkThe three dimensions every competency is formed and assessed across: what you know, who you are becoming, and what you can do. by a mentor teamDefined once · across the networkA small group who knows the person and confirms they can do the work, in place -- the unit of assessment, not a lone examiner. that knows the person's real context. What a leader shows is proficiencyDefined once · across the networkDemonstrated capacity in a real setting -- what a person can actually do, shown, not what they sat through. in the place they serve, not a transcript of hours.
