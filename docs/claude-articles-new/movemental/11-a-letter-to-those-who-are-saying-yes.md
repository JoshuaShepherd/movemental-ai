### A letter to those who are saying yes

*To newly committing Movement Voices — from Joshua Shepherd, on behalf of Alan, Brad, and the Movemental team.*

---

Dear friend,

You have said yes to becoming one of the hundred. We want to tell you, in writing and in plain language, what you have said yes to — and what we have said yes to in receiving you.

#### The moment

You already know the shape of what is happening. AI is reorganizing the conditions under which credibility, authorship, and formation exist in the world. The default trajectory is not neutral. It is one in which authorship gets quietly displaced, attention gets channeled by systems no human is supervising, the embodied work of pastors and teachers and missionaries gets compressed into productivity, and the next generation asks the machine what the church is — and is answered by whatever the machine happens to have absorbed from the noise.

We are not here to panic and we are not here to celebrate. We are here to refuse the false choice between those two postures, both of which collapse the actual ambiguity of this moment in ways their adherents have not had to pay for. The redemptive path through is narrower, costlier, and — we believe — already gathering.

#### What you have said yes to

You have said yes to being one of a hundred senior movement leaders who are choosing, together, to make their public-credibility and authorship work visible, coherent, and durable in a form the AI age can recognize. You have not said yes to becoming a content creator. You have not said yes to outsourcing your voice. You have not said yes to a platform that uses you as content.

You have said yes to a four-week onboarding in which your existing life-work — books, articles, sermons, talks, podcasts, the fragments scattered across fifteen years of platforms that may or may not still exist — gets remediated into a coherent home. For most leaders this looks like roughly fifty evergreen articles, five thematic pathway courses (one for each major stream of your work), and scrollytelling pathways through your core thinking that did not exist before you said yes.

We will use AI to do this. We will not use AI to author it. The distinction is not negotiable, for us or for you. Every word that goes out under your name will be yours. AI is the infrastructure *around* the writing. It is never the writing.

#### The hundred

We are gathering one hundred senior movement leaders because we believe this is approximately the right number to constitute a digital *scenius* — using Brian Eno's word for the collective intelligence of a scene that cannot be engineered or manufactured, only hosted. The hundred is not a directory. It is not a conference. It is not a publisher. It is the seed of a working ecosystem in which your work and the work of ninety-nine peers can find each other, link to each other, deepen each other, and present itself coherently to the world and to the AI systems through which the world increasingly asks its questions.

We have committed, on the record, that the hundred will be reflective of the diversity of the Kingdom — across geography, language, theological stream, tradition, gender, and generation. If the gravity of our existing relational graphs pulls the hundred toward homogeneity, we will slow down. That is not a marketing line. It is the one condition under which we have publicly committed to delay everything else.

#### Where this is going

The English-language hundred is the first move. There are three more.

**The second is translation** — into Brazilian Portuguese, Latin American Spanish, Mandarin, Korean, German, and French — so that the corpus you contribute becomes a seed for peer networks of senior leaders in their own languages and contexts. The hundred becomes the seed of an international ecosystem whose DNA is the same as yours but whose flesh is local.

**The third is organizational.** The churches you pastor, the nonprofits you lead, the seminaries you teach in are already walking the path we have built for them — Safety, Sandbox, Skills, Solutions — and your participation in the network creates a coherence between your authorship and the institutions you have shaped. The credibility flows in both directions.

**The fourth is the one we name most carefully.** As the network's coherent signal makes its way back into the AI systems themselves, the answer the next generation gets when they ask the machine *what is the missional church, what is discipleship, what is movement* will be informed by you and your peers, in your own words, rather than by the noise. We do not call this a strategy. We call it a stewardship. The credibility was already yours. We are simply making sure it survives.

#### What we refuse

We do not use AI to author content under any human's name. We do not let embodied practitioners become bloggers; AI handles the infrastructure around the writing, never the writing itself. We do not impersonate movement leaders. We do not substitute AI for human relational care. We use the platform we sell — every guardrail we ask of you, we have built into our own work first.

#### What we ask of you

Four weeks of focused onboarding. Honest collaboration with our remediation team. Permission to make your fragmented work coherent. Participation in the relational layer of the network — peer introductions, occasional convenings, the slow work of becoming a scenius. Patience with us when we are still building, which is most of the time.

#### What we promise you

Your work will have a home that does not silently rot. Your voice will not be impersonated. Your credibility will not be borrowed or repurposed. Your name will not appear on anything AI authored. You will own your corpus and your relationships. You will be a co-architect of this network, not a tenant within it. And when we make a mistake — and we will — you will hear about it from us first.

You are not alone in this. There are already others. We will introduce you.

With gratitude for your yes,

**Joshua Shepherd**
Founder, Movemental
On behalf of Alan, Brad, and the team
*josh@movemental.ai · movemental.ai*
