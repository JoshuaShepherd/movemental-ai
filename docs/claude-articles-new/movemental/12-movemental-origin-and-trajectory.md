### Movemental — Origin & Trajectory

*An onboarding synthesis, drawn from a recorded conversation with Joshua Shepherd. Captures what is most distinctive about the heart and ten-year picture of the work — meant to live alongside the methodology in the field guides, not replace it.*

---

#### Where it began

Movemental was born in a season of grief and gift held together. In 2025, Joshua's father died — closing a long arc of a father-shaped hole that had been forming for years before the physical loss. In the same season, two of Joshua's heroes — Alan Hirsch and Brad Brisco, whose work he had lived inside for fifteen years — were already two years into conversation with him about authorship, credibility, and what AI was about to do to both. The first call with Alan had been recorded because Joshua knew, before either of them said it aloud, that it was a milestone. His wife was waiting upstairs.

What followed was not strategy. It was a cascade of "couldn't nots."

Joshua couldn't not build something for Alan — a custom platform that, with the speed of context coding, could be his CMS, LMS, e-commerce, subscription engine, and AI agent layer at once. A week in, he couldn't not see that Brad needed the same architecture. Then that the roughly one hundred senior movement leaders Alan and Brad already moved among needed it. Then that the churches, nonprofits, and seminaries those leaders served needed the same pieces. Then that the AI moment didn't just permit a rebuild — it *required* one. And then that a technological rebuild without a human formation rebuild would be malpractice. Each "couldn't not" widened the circle. None of them were chosen. All of them were discovered.

When Joshua names what was actually fueling this, he names love — of making, of translation, of language and design, of the community with Alan and Brad, of the recognition of Jesus inside the work, and of *the ability to consciously transmit longing and sorrow into building and creating.* That line is load-bearing. **Movemental is grief turned into infrastructure.**

---

#### What we are actually building

The most important word we have for it now is **scenius** — Brian Eno's term for the collective intelligence of a scene that cannot be engineered or manufactured, only hosted. There is an embodied movement of senior leaders already in the world. They are local, sent, geographically scattered, and unable to uproot. What they have lacked is a *digital scenius* adequate to the public-credibility and authorship dimension of their callings.

Movemental, then, is a four-layer thing, and it matters not to confuse the layers:

- The **scenius** is the thing being served — the collective relational, intellectual, and practical intelligence of the network. It is not the platform. It is not ours to own.
- The **corpus** is the authors' own work — their books, articles, sermons, talks, podcasts — owned by them, remediated with their consent.
- The **platform** is the infrastructure that lets a distributed scene act like a scene — CMS, LMS, cohort engine, multi-tenant linking, translation layer.
- **AI** is the interpretive mirror — the only technology now capable of reading millions of fragmented pre-existing words (the Goodreads comments, the Amazon reviews, the bios, the talks) alongside the corpus and reflecting the network back to itself coherently.

The hundred is the seed. Four-week onboarding remediates each leader's life-work into a coherent shape — roughly fifty evergreen articles and five thematic pathway courses per leader, scaled across the network. Courses cannot be self-served; they run in cohort, with facilitated dissonance, action, and reflection, anchored to embodied local accountability.

---

#### What we believe will be true ten years from now

If the path runs, by year ten:

- The hundred has assembled — and held its commitment to be reflective of the diversity of the Kingdom, against the gravity of the founders' existing relational graphs. *Joshua has named this, on the record, as the one condition under which he would slow the entire thing down.* That commitment now has teeth.
- The corpus has been translated into Brazilian Portuguese, Latin American Spanish, Mandarin, Korean, German, and French — turning a hundred English-speaking seniors into the seed of an international ecosystem with peer networks that mirror the original DNA in their own contexts.
- Organizational multiplication has happened downstream: the churches these leaders pastor, the nonprofits they lead, and the seminaries they teach in have walked the Safety → Sandbox → Skills → Solutions path, governed by their own Guidebooks and formed people. Miniature seniors networks have formed on the same blueprint.
- The network's coherent signal back into the LLM substrate has reclaimed authorship of how movement thinking gets answered when the next generation asks an AI "what is the missional church." This is the GEO move — and it is downstream of credibility, not a substitute for it.
- The formation capacities developed for senior leaders are being pointed at the next generation — particularly young adults seeking a redemptive vocational formation in the AI age.

This is not "use AI to do mission work faster." This is **prophetic infrastructure** — building the alternative digital commons that mission work requires in order to survive what AI is about to do to credibility, authorship, and formation.

---

#### What we refuse

We do not use AI to author content under a human's name. We do not let embodied practitioners become bloggers; AI is the infrastructure *around* the writing, never the writing itself. We refuse both the techno-utopian and the Luddite scripts, because both collapse the ambiguity of this moment in ways that haven't cost their adherents anything. We live in the tension. We use the platform we sell.

---

#### The named vulnerability

Joshua has named, on the record, the formation risk most particular to this configuration: the collapse of identity into vocation when the alignment between a leader's life and the work is unusually perfect. He has asked the community to help him watch for the early signs — and to name them before they become late ones. This document is the first artifact of that asking.
