The Reality Assessment

Find out what is actually happening, before you decide what should.

Your leaders predict how AI is being used across your organization. Your people answer the same questions anonymously. You see the two side by side. There is no score and no grade, only the gap.

  • Free, and you can run it again whenever you like.
  • No one’s individual answers are ever shown, to anyone.
  • Results appear only once at least five people have answered.

Staff, and volunteers or board where it fits, answer the same questions about themselves. Anonymously, and without seeing what leaders predicted.
