# MOVEMENTAL.AI — HOMEPAGE REVISION PROMPT

## Objective

Create a complete, high-fidelity homepage mockup for **Movemental.ai**, building directly upon the existing homepage design shown in the supplied screenshots.

This is an editorial and conversion-focused refinement of the current design, not a rebrand.

The existing website already has a compelling aesthetic, strong philosophical foundations, and distinctive positioning. Preserve those strengths while making the company's institutional offering significantly clearer.

**Primary design objective:** A visitor should understand within the first 30 seconds:

1. Movemental helps churches, nonprofits, networks, and institutions lead their organizational response to AI.
2. Movemental believes that human convictions, responsibilities, and institutional authority should guide technological adoption—not the other way around.
3. Movemental offers a practical methodology called **the Map**, bringing together organizational decisions, knowledge, formation, implementation, and ongoing stewardship.
4. Movemental can actually build and manage the custom AI-enabled platforms, knowledge environments, and tools necessary to put those decisions into practice.
5. Movemental is grounded in a real network of experienced movement leaders and practitioners, not simply technology consultants selling generic AI solutions.

The site should appeal simultaneously to an individual wondering how their organization should respond to AI and an executive director, pastor, president, or organizational decision-maker evaluating Movemental as a serious institutional partner.

The experience must accommodate both audiences without forcing them into one journey.

- Purple AI illustrations.

The site should feel human, intellectual, credible, contemplative, and technically capable.

Responsive behavior must be considered, with strong mobile typography, natural stacking of editorial columns, and accessible controls.
